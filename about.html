About This Portal

This portal visualizes water quality monitoring data from Laguna de Bay, the largest freshwater lake in the Philippines, managed by the Laguna Lake Development Authority (LLDA). All parameter thresholds follow DENR Administrative Order 2016-08 Class C standards.

Laguna Lake Development Authority (LLDA)

The LLDA is the government agency mandated to manage, preserve, and develop Laguna de Bay and its watershed. It conducts regular water quality monitoring across the lake's major bays to track compliance with national environmental standards and to guide regulatory action.

Water Quality Parameters

DENR DAO 2016-08 Class C Standards

DENR Administrative Order No. 2016-08 establishes Water Quality Guidelines and General Effluent Standards for the Philippines. Class C waters are designated for fisheries, recreation (Class II), and other inland waters — which applies to Laguna de Bay. This portal evaluates all monitored parameters against these Class C thresholds.

Data Methodology

Representative Sample Data

The water quality values displayed in this portal are representative sample data modeled after documented conditions in Laguna de Bay. They are not live sensor readings. Station coordinates and bay designations are based on LLDA monitoring station references. Trend data covers a simulated six-month period (June–November). The lake boundary polygon is sourced from OpenStreetMap (relation 79348).
