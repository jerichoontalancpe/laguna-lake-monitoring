About This Portal

This portal visualizes water quality monitoring data from Laguna de Bay, the largest freshwater lake in the Philippines, managed by the Laguna Lake Development Authority (LLDA). All parameter thresholds follow the Class C guidelines of DENR Administrative Orders 2016-08 and 2021-19.

Laguna Lake Development Authority (LLDA)

The LLDA is the government agency mandated to manage, preserve, and develop Laguna de Bay and its watershed. It conducts regular water quality monitoring across the lake's major bays to track compliance with national environmental standards and to guide regulatory action.

Tributary River Stations

In addition to the 16 lake stations, the LLDA continuously monitors 36 tributary river stations across rivers draining into Laguna de Bay — including Marikina, San Pedro, Biñan, Sta. Rosa, Cabuyao, Los Baños, Pagsanjan, Binangonan, Angono, Morong, and others. This portal visualizes only the 16 active lake stations using Q2 2026 (April–June 2026) data.

DENR DAO 2016-08 Class C Standards

DENR Administrative Order No. 2016-08 establishes Water Quality Guidelines and General Effluent Standards for the Philippines. Laguna de Bay is classified as Class C water, intended mainly for fishery use. DAO 2021-19 later updated the guideline values for selected parameters, including ammonia, phosphate and fecal coliform. This portal evaluates every reading against the Class C value.

Parameter Reference

Status Evaluation Logic

Pass Good

The reading meets the Class C guideline value, the classification of Laguna de Bay.

Alert Fair

The reading misses the Class C value but stays within the Class D range, the next lower water class.

Fail Poor

The reading is beyond even the Class D range; the report marks these values in black.

Data Methodology

LLDA Q2 2026 Monitoring Data

All readings come from the LLDA Quarterly Water Quality Monitoring Report for April to June 2026 (Environmental Laboratory and Research Division). The map and station status use the June 2026 reading; the trend charts plot the April, May and June monthly readings. Values reported as “<0.1” are below the laboratory detection limit and are shown as reported. Station coordinates are approximate positions within each named bay, because the report lists station names but not coordinates. The lake boundary polygon is from OpenStreetMap (relation 79348).

The map, charts and table focus on 6 core parameters (DO, BOD, pH, Nitrate, Phosphate, TSS). Ammonia, Chloride and Fecal Coliform are shown in station details, and all 9 count toward each station’s status. Limits follow DENR DAO 2016-08, and DAO 2021-19 for ammonia, phosphate and fecal coliform. Archived Stations III, VI and VII are not in the Q2 2026 report and have no readings.
